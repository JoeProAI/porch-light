## Meterless engine

Scout Intent gates the local call-script path: classify, bind the CCN, sign a contract, refuse an unsigned or expired contract before a provider call. The published explanation batches are not on that gate. H-MEM, World Model, and Markovian are not claimed.

## Nemotron route

Nightshift on the operator machine is `http://127.0.0.1:8088/v1`. The NVIDIA key stays in Nightshift Providers. Thinking stays off. The hosted desk does not call Nightshift when you open a county. It shows the drafts the sweep already stored.

Local GPU inference is not a result in this submission. The offline probe status is `blocked_preflight`.

## Open data

NWS, IEM, CMS, the Census Bureau, CDC/ATSDR, and HHS emPOWER keep their own licenses in `NOTICE` and in the provenance sidecars. They are not event sponsors.
