## Zetaris, in practice

Local Docker from the licensed bundle. JDBC port `10000`. The datasource name is `porchlight_postgis`. One physical PostGIS database holds the projections. The hosted desk serves the frozen audit. It does not open JDBC from the browser. A multi-system Unified Semantic Layer is not claimed.

## Meterless and Nemotron, in practice

Nightshift on the operator machine at `http://127.0.0.1:8088/v1`. Hosted county sheets show drafts that already ran: 14,960 passed and 480 failed in the published pack. `execution_location` is `nvidia_cloud`. `paid_fallback_enabled` is false across that pack.

Scout Intent is the engine this build uses. The retained $0 script is one facility, CCN `102853`, receipt `rcpt_req_qsa07grv`. Batch explanation drafts are not contract-gated.

H-MEM, World Model, and Markovian are not in the product. Local GPU, offline DGX, Content Safety, and Parse are not demonstrated.

`engines/openrouter_backup.py` is an optional module. It stays off unless `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` and `PORCHLIGHT_OPENROUTER_BACKUP` are set. It is not a scoring sponsor and no spend is claimed.

## Cursor, in practice

Status in `metadata.json` is `used`. The evidence is the merged pull requests above and the rules in `.cursor/rules/`. This repository has no Cursor token count and no spend total. Do not invent one.

## Open data, not sponsors

NWS, IEM, CMS, the U.S. Census Bureau, CDC/ATSDR, and HHS emPOWER are sources. Their attribution stays on the provenance sidecars and in `NOTICE`. They are not event sponsors and not partner integrations.
