Porch Light is the call sheet you open when a county needs an order of outreach for dialysis centers and nursing homes. The live desk is [porchlight.earth](https://porchlight.earth). Home leads with **Pick any US county**. Hurricane Milton stays on a footer link.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/hero-key-art.webp" alt="Night storm over land with one amber porch light on. Project key art, not a satellite image and not a live warning." />
</Frame>

You pick a county. You get that county's ranked facilities, directory phones, score terms, refusals, and a Nemotron draft where the published snapshot has one. You record a contact outcome on your own device if you actually made a call. The app does not place calls or send messages.

The ranking formula is a published prototype. It is not a clinical order. Current facility conditions are not verified.

## What you can say

Three claims, and the counts underneath them:

1. Every county in the 3,235-row Census catalog has been source-audited through Zetaris at least once. The source report is `engines/reports/national-sweep-2026-10-23.md`. After a separate retry for Robertson County, Texas (`48395`), 2,491 counties return ranked rows.
2. Grounded drafts are in progress on free Nemotron routes through Meterless. The hosted pack, generated `2026-10-24T16:47:04Z`, contains 14,960 passed facility explanations and 480 failed ones across 15,440 ranked rows. 89 counties with ranked rows have only failed drafts. Anchorage is one of them.
3. You can open any catalog county on the live desk today. Counties with ranked rows open that published snapshot. Counties with no rankable rows open as an empty, sourced result. Robertson has no snapshot file: the live county route returns 503 and does not substitute Manatee.

Leave these out of the recording and the submission text: an offline or local DGX run, H-MEM, World Model, Markovian, drafts for every county, a reach-out you did not make, and Manatee as the product.

## Numbers to keep apart

Counted from the committed catalog and county snapshots on 2026-10-25. The live `GET /api/national` body matched this catalog (3,235 rows, the same outcome counts, sweep `20261023T200048Z-78a426ba`).

| Count | Value | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Catalog rows | 3,235 | Zetaris catalog query `833`, `2026-10-23T20:00:50Z` |
| Hosted snapshots | 3,234 | `web/data/national/published/counties/` |
| Hosted counties with ranked rows | 2,490 | Catalog outcome `complete_with_ranked_rows` |
| No matching CMS rows | 353 | Catalog outcome `complete_no_matching_source_rows` |
| CMS rows, none rankable | 391 | Catalog outcome `complete_no_rankable_rows` |
| First-pass runtime error | 1 | Robertson `48395`. Live read returns no substitute sheet |
| Ranked facility rows in the pack | 15,440 | Sum of `ranked_rows` in the catalog |
| Passed drafts in the pack | 14,960 | `model_explanation.status` `passed` |
| Failed drafts in the pack | 480 | `model_explanation.status` `failed` |

The 2,491 ranked-county figure includes the Robertson retry. That retry is not in the hosted pack. Say 2,490 when you are pointing at porchlight.earth. Say 2,491 only when you are pointing at the source report's recovery note.

A county with zero CMS rows is not proof that no care facility exists there.
