Porch Light is an emergency-operations call sheet for dialysis centers and nursing homes in any US county. You pick a county. A fixed formula ranks the facilities that joined the audited sources. Nemotron writes the reason. Records that did not join stay on the sheet.

The live desk is [porchlight.earth/nationwide](https://porchlight.earth/nationwide). Home says **Pick any US county** and opens that desk. The page title is `Porch Light | Pick any US county.`

## What you can say

Read on the live home, 2026-10-26.

<Check>
Every catalog county has been source-audited. An empty county stays empty. Nothing is filled in from another county.
</Check>

<Check>
Drafts are in progress on free Nemotron routes. Not every county has an accepted draft. A failed draft stays failed.
</Check>

<Check>
An operator can open any published county that has ranked facilities, today, on the live desk.
</Check>

The formula ranks. Nemotron does not rank. Do not claim an offline DGX run, H-MEM, or a World Model.
