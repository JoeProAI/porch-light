You open a US county. Porch Light shows the dialysis centers and nursing homes that joined the audited sources, in an order a fixed formula set, with directory phones and the facts that did not join.

The live desk is [porchlight.earth](https://porchlight.earth). Home says **Pick any US county** and goes to `/nationwide`. The Hurricane Milton sheet is a footer link, **Hurricane Milton retrospective**. It is a saved example. It is not the product.

That retry file is not in the hosted pack. Quote 2,490 when you mean the desk a judge can open. Quote 2,491 only when you mean that report's post-retry source count.

## Two sheets, on purpose

| Sheet | Route | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published national county | `/nationwide?county=` then the call sheet | Frozen audited sweep snapshot. National weights `national-source-screen-v1`. No county plan term |
| Hurricane Milton retrospective | `/operate` with no county query | Saved Manatee sheet: 18 ranked rows, 2 refusals, weights `manatee-development-v1`, including an archived plan term |

The national snapshot for Manatee (`12081`) is a different sheet: 14 ranked and 6 refused. Do not describe that national row count as the Milton walkthrough.
