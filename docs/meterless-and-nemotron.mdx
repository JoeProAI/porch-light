## Not this path

Parse, Content Safety, and embeddings are not demonstrated. The offline Nightshift probe stopped at `blocked_preflight` with no generation (`engines/reports/nemotron-offline-proof-2026-10-23.md`).

`engines/openrouter_backup.py` can retry one failed `call_script` through OpenRouter on the same Lightning id, only if `PORCHLIGHT_OPENROUTER_BACKUP` and `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` are set, under `OPENROUTER_MAX_SPEND_USD`. The published executions did not take that path. Leave it off.

H-MEM, World Model, and Markovian are not engines in `metadata.json`. The engines list is Scout Intent.
