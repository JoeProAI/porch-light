## How a county job works

1. Search the Census county catalog (3,235 rows in the catalog path) or click a county on the night atlas.
2. `POST /api/national` starts `engines.national_jobs` for that FIPS (or an alert + capture pair).
3. Poll the job. On success, open the call sheet for **that** run's audited rows and source gaps.
4. If Zetaris cannot be reached, the UI says so. It does not load Manatee as a silent fallback for a bad FIPS.

## Verified local runs (examples)

Completed jobs have existed on the operator machine for counties such as Los Angeles CA, Cook IL, Harris TX, Maricopa AZ, and Denver CO, plus Manatee. Treat those as **local machine receipts**, not as a finished national product. Catalog size ≠ completed call sheets for every county.

## Honesty rules

- Do not market “3,000+ counties ready” until the sweep and offline replay actually cover them.
- Contact outcomes stay operator-entered. No sample **Reached** rows.
- Alert polygon jobs and single-county jobs are different engine inputs. The merged national web work is [PR #24](https://github.com/JoeProAI/porch-light/pull/24); engine notes are in `engines/README.md`.
