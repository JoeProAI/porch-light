The call sheet is built from public facility records and public geographic context. Provenance sits on the capture. The app does not treat a directory listing as proof that a facility is open, staffed, or inside today's warning.

Source names and URLs below are the entries in `data/adapters/us/sources.yaml`. National bytes for the full catalog live in the private filestore. The checked-in raw profile in `data/snapshot/raw/` is the Manatee intake plus provenance. The hosted county pack is the audited result of the national capture, not a second copy of every raw file.

## Facilities

| Source | What it contributes | Vintage noted in the repo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CMS nursing homes, dataset `4pq5-n9py` | Facility identity, county, phone, star rating | Provider-data release modified `2026-09-30` |
| CMS dialysis, dataset `23ew-n7w9` | Facility identity and address for geocoding | Provider-data release modified `2026-06-16` |

The national capture accounts for 22,180 distinct CMS records in the source report, including refusals and county-join gaps. The first pass of the sweep placed 15,440 of those into ranked rows and recorded 6,460 refusals. Names beginning `zz_closed_` stay refused (`cms_source_marked_closed`). A CMS county label is not, by itself, an exact geocode.

Phones on ranked rows are the CMS directory numbers that passed the digit check. They are not verified emergency contacts. The data-quality rule `emergency_contact_verified` stays false.

## Geography

Census geocoder, benchmark `Public_AR_Current`, vintage `Census2020_Current`: `https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/geographies/address`.

A ranked row needs `match_status` `Match` and `match_type` `Exact`, and the source point covered by official county geometry. Any other match is refused: `census_address_match_not_exact`, `census_address_not_matched`, or `source_point_not_covered_by_current_county_geometry`. An absent Exact rating is not stored as Exact.

The catalog is the loaded NWS county file joined to the latest national capture: 3,235 county and county-equivalent rows, query id `833`. That includes Connecticut planning regions (FIPS prefix `09`, 9 rows) and territory equivalents: American Samoa 5, Guam 1, Northern Mariana Islands 4, Puerto Rico 78, U.S. Virgin Islands 3. Those 100 rows are in the published catalog. They were not skipped.

NWS zone shapes and the IEM archive supply warning geometry for alert jobs and for the Milton retrospective. Current published shapes are not asserted to match October 2024 boundaries. `historical_2024_boundary_equivalence_verified` is false on both the national snapshots and the Milton sheet.

## Vulnerability and electricity-dependent equipment

CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022, tract file, joined on the Census tract GEOID. National percentiles are used. Unavailable percentiles stay unavailable. The SVI `data_as_of` flag on a national row is stale because the vintage is 2022.

HHS emPOWER public FeatureServer, ZIP layer 1, electricity-dependent durable medical equipment. The count is Medicare beneficiaries in that ZIP who use that equipment. It is not a count of patients at the facility on the row. Masked source values of 11 are refused (`ambiguous_or_masked_source_count_11`). A missing ZIP context is a refusal, not a zero.

## Weather

`https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active` is the live alert URL. On porchlight.earth the catalog's weather block is `unavailable` and points you at that official URL. Local origin can refresh a capture and run an alert through the counties the official geometry maps. Unmapped alerts, including marine zones, return a source gap. The Doña Ana flash-flood check in `docs/smoke-national-results.md` ranked zero facilities inside the polygon even though the county sheet ranks seven. The alert sheet did not reuse the county list.

Storm Watch (`/watch`) shows captured official alerts and radar frames for the regions that have frames. A missing frame stays missing.

## County plans

The Milton path can cite the Bradenton adoption PDF of the Manatee CEMP captured under `data/snapshot/raw/cemp/`. Applicability to October 2024 is unverified. The national screen does not import that PDF, does not guess a plan for any other county, and does not encode a missing plan as zero.

## Provenance

Ingest writes a `.provenance.json` sidecar: source URL, `fetched_at` UTC, SHA-256, license line, fetch command. The published catalog and each county gzip have sidecars. The national source capture id on the hosted catalog is `47344192d80249d0c96a69fb2acd0f11a6b32009a8cd0607dca8872609488ac0`.
