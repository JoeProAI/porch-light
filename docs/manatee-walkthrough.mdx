## Not the same sheet as hosted Manatee

`/nationwide?county=12081` is the national source screen, generated `2026-10-23T22:35:28Z`: 20 source rows, 14 ranked, 6 refused, 14 passed drafts, formula `national-source-screen-v1`, no county-plan term. If a link adds `?county=` or `?alert=` to `/operate`, the app redirects to `/nationwide` and will not mount this Milton file.

## Why it exists

The Milton file is a real-source snapshot with a warning identity, an evidence drawer, and operator contact state. It is the retrospective example. It is not a template. Other counties do not inherit its plan, its formula, or its 18 rows.
