`/operate` with no county query is the Hurricane Milton retrospective for Manatee County, Florida. On the live home it is a 12px footer link, **Hurricane Milton retrospective**. The amber action on home is **Pick any US county**.

<Warning>
Do not present this sheet as nationwide coverage. The national published snapshot for the same FIPS is a different run, with a different formula and different row counts.
</Warning>

## How to read it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open /operate">
    No `county` query. A county or alert query on `/operate` redirects to `/nationwide` so a bad FIPS cannot fall through to this sheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the warning label">
    The eyebrow is the retrospective Milton label, not a live warning. Boundary equivalence to the 2024 zone is unverified (`historical_2024_boundary_equivalence_verified` is false).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open rank 1">
    Read the five score terms, the Zetaris query ids, and the model block. A failed row stays failed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave contact status on Not contacted">
    Change it only after a real call. Export or print if you need a paper copy. Nothing auto-dials.
  </Step>
</Steps>
